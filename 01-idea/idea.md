# Idea

**Working name:** RideMood

**Idea sentence:** RideMood helps Budapest public transport passengers check and report how comfortable a vehicle is (AC, crowding, cleanliness), so summer trips are less of a torture.

**Main task:** Check how comfortable a vehicle is before boarding. On the same screen, passengers can also report the vehicle's condition while riding. The app runs fully on user reports.

## User group
Public transport users in Budapest: my family, friends and girlfriend. 5 people, all reachable in person or by message.

## Problem hypothesis
In summer, passengers can't know which vehicle is hot or crowded, and the first version of my app was too confusing to use quickly. I will test this in the interviews and observations.

## Platform
Web and mobile, mobile first. Phones are the easiest way to use it on the road.

## What I will not do
1. A cluttered interface
2. Small text
3. Big ads
